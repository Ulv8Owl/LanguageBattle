1.1 "I get up" — Present Simple for a daily habit. "Get up" means "leave the bed"; "wake up" means "stop sleeping".
1.2 "at seven" — We use "at" with clock time: at seven, at 7:30.
1.3 "every morning" — This says how often. It usually goes at the end of the sentence.
1.4 "Then I make" — "Then" = after that. It joins the second sentence to the first one.
1.5 "coffee" — Uncountable noun: no "a", no plural. We say "make coffee".
1.6 "and read the news" — Same subject "I", so we do not repeat it. "News" is always with "the" and takes a singular verb.
2.1 "My office" — "My" replaces the article. Never say "the my office".
2.2 "is near" — "Near" is a preposition here. Do not add "to": near the station.
2.3 "the train station" — "The" because it is one known station. "Train station" is one noun made of two words.
2.4 "I walk" — Present Simple. "Walk" = go on foot.
2.5 "there" — "There" replaces "to the train station". Never say "to there".
2.6 "in ten minutes" — "In + time" says how long the action takes.
3.1 "I don't like" — Negative Present Simple: "don't" + verb without "s".
3.2 "cold weather" — "Weather" is uncountable: no "a", no plural.
3.3 ", so I stay at home" — "So" shows the result. "At home" has no article.
3.4 "Today" — A time word. It can stand at the beginning of the sentence.
3.5 "it is" — English needs a subject, so we use "it" for the weather.
3.6 "very windy" — "Windy" is an adjective from the noun "wind". "Very" makes it stronger.
4.1 "My brother" — Third person singular subject (= he).
4.2 "works" — We add "-s" because the subject is he / she / it.
4.3 "in a big hotel" — "In" for buildings. "A" because we do not know which hotel.
4.4 "He starts" — "He" replaces "my brother". "-s" again.
4.5 "work" — Here "work" is a noun and it is uncountable: no article.
4.6 "at six" — "At" + clock time, the same as "at seven" in line 1.
5.1 "We have" — With "we" we use "have", not "has".
5.2 "a small dog" — The adjective goes before the noun: a small dog, not "a dog small".
5.3 ", and he sleeps a lot" — "A lot" = very much. It goes at the end.
5.4 "In the evening" — Fixed phrase with "in the". Compare: "at night".
5.5 "we walk" — Here "walk" means "go for a walk".
5.6 "in the park" — "In" + place. "The" because it is our usual park.
6.1 "I am" — The verb "be" with "I" is "am".
6.2 "a student" — We use "a" with jobs and roles in the singular.
6.3 ", and I study Spanish" — Languages have a capital letter and no article.
6.4 "My class" — Singular noun, so the verb after it is "is".
6.5 "is" — "Be" for a timetable or a fixed plan.
6.6 "on Monday and Thursday" — "On" + days of the week.
7.1 "This shop" — "This" = the one here, near me.
7.2 "is open" — "Open" is an adjective here, so we need "is".
7.3 "every day" — Two separate words. It says how often.
7.4 "I buy" — Present Simple for a repeated action.
7.5 "bread and milk" — Both are uncountable: no "a", no plural.
7.6 "here" — "Here" = in this shop. No preposition before it.
8.1 "My phone" — "My" instead of an article again.
8.2 "is very old" — "Very" + adjective after the verb "be".
8.3 ", so it is slow" — "So" shows the result of the first part.
8.4 "I want" — After "want" we use a noun or "to + verb", never "-ing".
8.5 "a new one" — "One" = phone. We use it so we do not repeat the noun.
8.6 "next year" — No preposition with "next": say "next year", not "in next year".
9.1 "On Saturday" — "On" + day of the week.
9.2 "I clean" — Present Simple for a regular action every Saturday.
9.3 "my room" — Possessive word, no article with it.
9.4 "After that" — It links to the first sentence: after cleaning the room.
9.5 "I cook" — "Cook" = make hot food.
9.6 "lunch for my family" — Meals take no article: cook lunch. "For" + person.
10.1 "I don't drink" — "Don't" + the main verb without "-s".
10.2 "coffee" — Uncountable, so no article, as in line 1.
10.3 "in the evening" — The same fixed phrase as in line 5.
10.4 "It is bad" — "It" here means "drinking coffee in the evening".
10.5 "for my sleep" — "Bad for" + noun is a fixed pattern.
10.6 ", so I drink tea" — "So" = that is why. "Tea" is uncountable: no article.
